OneUp Apparel Commercial (Spec Work)

Overview

OneUp is a self-initiated spec apparel brand I created. The brand, concept, apparel designs, commercial, script, narration direction, sound design, editing, and overall creative vision were developed independently. AI-generated footage was used to bring the commercial to life, as I did not have the production resources to capture the footage myself. The shots, visual direction, and intended performances were all conceptualized by me, with the generated footage serving as the production medium. The apparel designs featured in the commercial were also created by me.

The goal was to create more than a traditional apparel advertisement. I wanted to establish OneUp as a brand with a distinct personality—relatable, slightly self-deprecating, and motivational without taking itself too seriously. The commercial became an exercise in turning that philosophy into a piece of content that was entertaining to watch while still communicating where the brand came from.

Understanding The Brand

Since OneUp was a brand I created myself, I already had a clear understanding of its personality and philosophy before beginning the commercial. The brand was intentionally built around a more relatable form of motivation—one that doesn't take itself too seriously and avoids the typical tone associated with gym and fitness culture.

That distinction became important when approaching the commercial. I believe that people connect with people, which influences how I approach product-focused content. Rather than constantly putting the product in front of the viewer, I wanted the apparel to exist naturally within the story. The commercial needed to give people a reason to care about the brand first, with the product becoming part of that experience.

Creative Process

I wanted the commercial to establish OneUp within the first few seconds, then use the remaining time to tell the brand’s origin story without making it feel like a traditional brand explanation. The goal was to make the story entertaining enough that the audience experiences the brand’s personality rather than simply being told about it.

The narration became the foundation for the visual direction. Each line gave me an opportunity to either reinforce the idea directly or interpret it in an unexpected way. For example, when the narration says, “OneUp was formed from spontaneous thoughts,” the visual cuts to a group of people dressed as scientists gathered around a whiteboard covered in random phrases. It exaggerates the idea of spontaneous thinking and establishes the playful tone immediately.

I then grounded that exaggeration in experiences that feel familiar: “The ones you get in the shower. The ones you get at 3 a.m.” These moments are relatable because they reflect the kind of unexpected thoughts that inspired the brand. From there, I continued looking for ways to turn simple ideas into more visually interesting or humorous moments rather than illustrating them literally.

The “ones that sound odd... but are oddly motivating” section became an opportunity to accelerate the pacing. I created a rapid sequence of phrases appearing across different mediums—a billboard, a mirror, a notebook, and more. The notebook moment was deliberately different: it introduces OneUp’s signature phrase, then briefly slows the commercial down. The narration pauses and the sound drops away, giving the phrase a moment to land before the pace picks back up.

That contrast became an important part of the edit. Quick cuts, music, sound design, and humor keep the commercial energetic, while brief moments of silence and slower pacing create emphasis. I wanted the piece to move quickly without making every second feel equally intense.

The larger approach was simple: a brand story doesn't have to feel like a brand story. Instead of explaining what OneUp is, I wanted the audience to experience the kind of spontaneous thinking that created it.

Red Bull Brand Film (Spec Work)

Overview

This is a self-initiated spec project created for portfolio purposes only. Red Bull is not affiliated with this project, and all footage remains the property of its respective owners. The creative concept, brand analysis, script, narration, footage selection, sound design, and final edit were developed independently as an exercise in brand analysis and editorial storytelling.

Rather than recreating an existing advertisement, the objective was to create an original piece that felt authentic to Red Bull's identity while demonstrating my creative process from concept through to the final edit.

Understanding The Brand

The starting point wasn't footage, it was understanding what Red Bull actually communicates. Their marketing rarely focuses on the product itself. Instead, it sells a mindset built around ambition, exploration, and pushing beyond perceived limits.

That observation became the foundation of the project. Rather than creating a traditional product advertisement, the goal was to build an experience that reflected those values and established the brand's identity almost immediately.

Creative Process

My editing process rarely develops in a linear way. Instead of building an edit from beginning to end, I first establish the moments that will carry the greatest emotional weight, then shape everything else around them.

From the outset, one line never changed: "This is Red Bull." That opening immediately establishes the brand, allowing the rest of the piece to reinforce the message rather than explain it. From there, key moments were developed independently before being connected into a cohesive narrative. Once those anchors were established, every creative decision, from the script and footage selection to the pacing, music, and sound design, was made to strengthen them.

Felix Baumgartner's space jump was always intended to be the emotional climax. Knowing that destination early made it possible to gradually increase the intensity of the edit, with the narration, pacing, and visuals all building toward the phrase "the unknown" as Baumgartner steps from the capsule.

The editing style followed the same philosophy. With footage that already carried so much energy, I intentionally kept transitions and visual effects to a minimum. Rather than drawing attention to the edit itself, the focus remained on enhancing the story. Effects were only introduced where they strengthened a moment, allowing the action, pacing, and sound design to carry the momentum.

The ending received the same level of consideration. Rather than treating Red Bull's slogan as a standalone tagline, it became the conclusion of the final thought: "Red Bull doesn't just push limits... it gives you wings." Integrating the slogan into the narrative allowed it to feel like the natural resolution of the story instead of simply ending with a familiar marketing line.

Reflection

This project demonstrates the way I approach creative work as a whole. I enjoy analyzing why a brand communicates the way it does, identifying the moments that will have the greatest impact, and building everything else around those decisions. Rather than approaching editing as a technical exercise, I see it as a process of solving creative problems with intention.

Nike - The Silence Before The Start (Spec Work)

Overview

This is a self-initiated spec project created for portfolio purposes only. Nike is not affiliated with this project, and all footage remains the property of its respective owners. The creative concept, script, narration, footage selection, sound design, and final edit were developed independently as an exploration of introspective brand storytelling.

Rather than recreating a traditional sports advertisement, the objective was to create an original piece that reflected Nike's long-established focus on the psychological side of performance.

Understanding The Brand

What has always stood out to me about Nike is that their strongest campaigns rarely focus on the outcome, they focus on the battle that comes before it. Rather than celebrating victory, they explore uncertainty, fear, and self-doubt, making success feel earned rather than inevitable.

That observation became the foundation of the project.

Creative Process

The project didn't begin with visuals or pacing, it began with a contradiction.

The question I kept coming back to was: What happens when the audience hears "You're not fast enough" or "Not worthy enough" while looking at athletes who have already reached the highest level of their sport?

That tension became the driving force behind the entire piece. Pairing lines of self-doubt with figures like Cristiano Ronaldo creates an intentional disconnect between what the audience hears and what they see. Rather than feeling contradictory, it reinforces the idea that self-doubt doesn't disappear with success, it exists at every level.

Every creative decision was then made to support that idea. Presenting the edit entirely in black and white removed unnecessary distraction and reinforced the feeling of isolation and internal reflection. The pacing begins deliberately slow, allowing the narration to breathe before gradually shifting towards action.

Rather than treating Nike's slogan as a familiar tagline, the entire narrative was structured so it became the only logical conclusion. If thoughts don't prove anything and actions do, then the only answer is to take the first step and "Just Do It."

Reflection

One of the biggest lessons from this project was learning to trust simplicity. It reinforced that a strong idea doesn't need to be hidden behind constant effects or fast editing. If the concept is clear, every creative decision can work together to strengthen it without competing for attention.

NextRep Brand Awareness Campaign

Campaign Planning • Creative Strategy • Copywriting • Brand Consistency

Overview

As part of a university advertising project, I designed a series of five billboard advertisements for NextRep, a conceptual AI-powered training app created to bring professional-level performance insights to everyday athletes. The billboard series served as one component of a larger brand awareness campaign, introducing the brand through a sequence of connected advertisements rather than repeating a single message.

Objective

Design a cohesive billboard campaign that introduced the NextRep brand, generated awareness, and encouraged viewers to learn more through clear calls to action. Each billboard was intended to communicate a different aspect of the brand while maintaining a consistent visual identity across the series.

Design Approach

Rather than treating every billboard as the same advertisement in a different format, I designed each piece around its intended viewing environment. Highway billboards relied on concise messaging and prominent branding to capture attention in only a few seconds, while placements viewed for longer periods allowed for additional supporting information. Throughout the series, I prioritized readability, visual hierarchy, and minimal layouts to ensure each advertisement communicated effectively while remaining visually cohesive.

Deliverables

- Five billboard advertisements
- Advertising copy
- Brand-consistent visual layouts

Reflection

This project reinforced that effective advertising extends beyond creating visually appealing graphics. It challenged me to consider how location, viewing time, and audience behavior influence both design and messaging. By balancing consistency with location-specific adaptations, I gained a stronger understanding of how individual advertisements can work together to support a unified brand awareness campaign.

Overview

As part of a university social media marketing project, I developed and executed a simulated two-week integrated social media campaign for AirReel Assets, a conceptual drone cinematography brand specializing in aerial content creation for social media storytelling.

Rather than functioning as a standard promotional rollout, the campaign was designed as an interactive narrative experience. The audience was invited into the creative process of producing a final drone reel, with engagement-driven content that allowed users to actively influence creative decisions through platform-specific interactions. The goal was to create a sense of progression, anticipation, and participation over the full campaign period.

Objective

The primary objective of the campaign was to build brand awareness, credibility, and audience engagement by transforming passive viewers into active participants in the content creation process. Instead of only showcasing finished drone footage, the campaign emphasized transparency and collaboration, allowing audiences to feel involved in shaping the final output.

A key goal was also to strengthen cross-platform engagement by using each social media channel strategically, ensuring that content was not duplicated but purposefully distributed based on platform strengths. This approach was intended to demonstrate how a coordinated multi-platform strategy can increase engagement and drive users toward specific interactive touchpoints.

Campaign Strategy

A major component of the campaign was the deliberate segmentation of content across platforms, with each channel serving a distinct purpose within the overall ecosystem:

X (Twitter): Used as the primary engagement and decision-making hub. This platform hosted interactive polls that allowed users to vote on creative elements of the final drone reel, such as shot direction, themes, and visual preferences. It functioned as the central “interactive driver” of the campaign.
Instagram: Focused on behind-the-scenes content, visual storytelling, and aesthetic-driven posts. This included production snippets, teaser visuals, and creative process insights designed to build anticipation and emotional connection with the brand.
Facebook: Used for more traditional, structured promotional posts and campaign updates. This platform provided context, reinforced messaging, and supported longer-form explanations of what was happening across the campaign.

Each platform was intentionally interconnected, with content designed to guide users between channels. For example, Instagram and Facebook posts frequently directed audiences to X to participate in polls and influence the direction of the final drone reel. This created a unified cross-platform funnel that encouraged deeper engagement rather than isolated interaction.

Campaign Approach & Workflow

Before any content was produced, I developed a complete two-week campaign structure outlining the full content journey from start to finish. This included defining post objectives, sequencing content for narrative flow, and ensuring consistent messaging across all platforms.

A significant part of the process involved building a full content production workflow. This included:

- Content ideation and campaign planning
- Copywriting for captions tailored to each platform’s tone and audience behavior
- Designing platform-specific graphics and motion-based assets
- Structuring interactive poll content for engagement on X
- Creating behind-the-scenes visuals and teaser content
- Coordinating timing and scheduling for consistent daily posting

To manage execution, I used Adobe Express to schedule and automate the two-week campaign. Posts were strategically scheduled at consistent times each day, with typically two to three posts per day across platforms. This ensured a steady content rhythm while maintaining engagement momentum throughout the campaign duration.

The workflow emphasized consistency, planning, and narrative continuity, treating the campaign as a cohesive content system rather than isolated social media posts.

Deliverables

- 22 scheduled social media posts
- Campaign messaging and copywriting
- Platform-specific graphics and animations
- Interactive poll posts
- Behind-the-scenes content
- Final drone reel
- Two-week publishing schedule

Reflection

This project significantly strengthened my understanding of social media marketing as a structured, strategic process rather than isolated content creation. It reinforced the importance of building campaigns as interconnected systems where each platform plays a specific role in guiding audience behavior and engagement.

It also highlighted the value of pre-production planning, particularly in mapping content across time, platforms, and audience touchpoints before any assets are created. Managing the campaign through a full scheduling workflow helped demonstrate how consistency, timing, and platform-specific strategy contribute to a more cohesive and effective digital presence.
